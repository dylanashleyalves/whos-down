Who’s Down Privacy Terms

Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Who’s Down is an app for meeting people nearby by doing activities together. It’s run by Dylan Alves in Singapore (“we”). This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. We don’t sell your data and we don’t show ads.

What we collect

Your account

  • Email address, and a password if you sign up with email. Passwords are stored only in hashed form by our login provider; we can’t read them.
  • If you choose Sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture link with us. We only ask for this basic profile information.

Your profile

  • What you enter: display name, city, country, bio, interests and profile photo.
  • Date of birth, to make sure everyone is 18 or over.
  • Gender (guy, girl or prefer not to say), only used to decide whether you can see and join guys-only or girls-only activities.
  • When you accepted the community guidelines.

What you do in the app

  • Activities you post or join, and group chat messages.
  • Direct messages with your crew, and whether they’ve been read.
  • Who showed up to an activity (marked by the host), “Hang again?” choices, and the XP, ranks and stats worked out from them.
  • People you block, reports you make, and feedback you send.
  • When you last opened the app, and your notification settings.

Your device

  • A push notification token for your phone, if you allow notifications.

We don’t collect your precise location, contacts or advertising ID, and we don’t use analytics or tracking tools.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show activities in your country, let people join, power chats and messages, and work out ranks.
  • To send the notifications you’ve switched on: messages, people joining, reminders and occasional nudges.
  • To keep people safe: check ages, enforce the community guidelines and review reports. When someone files a report, the moderator gets an email about it.
  • To improve the app based on feedback you send.

Who can see what

  • Other users can see your profile (name, photo, city, country, bio, interests, rank and stats), activities you post or join, and messages in chats you’re part of.
  • Only you can see your email address, date of birth and gender.
  • The moderator can see reports, the reported content and feedback, and can remove activities or ban accounts.

Services we use

These companies process data for us, only to provide their service:

  • Supabase: database, login and photo storage.
  • Expo: app updates and delivering push notifications.
  • Google: Sign in with Google (if you use it), and Firebase Cloud Messaging, which delivers notifications on Android.
  • Resend: sends report alert emails to the moderator.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website.

These services may store data on servers outside Singapore.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. You can change most of it in the app at any time.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile → Settings → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, photos, activities, messages, crew and everything else linked to you. Backups held by our database provider are cleared on their normal schedule.

If you can’t open the app, contact us (below) and we’ll delete it for you once we’ve confirmed the account is yours.

Age

Who’s Down is only for people aged 18 and over. We don’t knowingly collect data from anyone younger, and we delete accounts we find that belong to under-18s.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or ask questions about this policy, by contacting us. We aim to reply within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. For bigger changes, we’ll also let you know in the app.

Contact

Use Profile → Settings → Send feedback in the app, or open an issue at github.com/dylanashleyalves/whos-down/issues.
