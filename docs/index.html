Who’s down?

Meet people nearby by doing things together. Post what you’re down for, see who joins, and turn one meetup into a crew.

Get the Android app

How it works

📣 Post a plan

Basketball tonight, coffee on Saturday, a gaming session. Pick a time and how many people you need.

🙋 People join

Others nearby tap “I’m down” and get a group chat to sort out the details.

🤝 Build your crew

After you meet, tap “Hang again?”. If they pick you too, you’re crew and can message any time.

A look inside

Safe by design

Everyone is 18 or over and agrees to community guidelines. You only see activities in your own country, people can report or block anyone, and you can delete your account from Settings at any time.
